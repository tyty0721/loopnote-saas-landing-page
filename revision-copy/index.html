FROM SCATTERED NOTES TO SHARED CONTEXT

Keep the conversation moving.

Three common meeting loose ends. One clearer way to follow through.

☷01

Find the point, not the paragraph.

Long notes make the important parts hard to find. A concise summary keeps the context together for the team.

A recap you can return to.
◇02

Make the decision visible.

Decisions get buried in conversation. A separate decision list makes the agreed direction easy to review.

The “what we agreed” stays clear.
✓03

Give every next step an owner.

“Someone should do this” leaves work hanging. Action items bring the task, owner, and due date into view.

Know who does what, and when.
