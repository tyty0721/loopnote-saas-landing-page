## Frequently asked questions

### Who is LoopNote designed for?

The concept is designed for project owners in remote or hybrid teams of 5–50 people. It focuses on making meeting outcomes and next steps easier to review.

### How is a summary different from an action item?

A summary explains the discussion and its context. An action item names a piece of follow-up work, its owner, and its due date. Decisions record what the team agreed.

### Does this website connect to Notion, Slack, or Jira?

No. These are target tools in the fictional product concept. The names describe the idea of a wider workflow; this demo has no live integrations.

### What is included in the Free plan?

The fictional Free plan is $0 with 5 meetings per month. The other displayed plans are Pro at $12 and Team at $29 per user per month. These are concept settings, not an available subscription.

### What data and functions does the demo use?

The demo uses three preset sample meetings. You can switch meetings, toggle task completion, and reset the examples. Changes stay in page memory and disappear on refresh. No real meeting is recorded, transcribed, or sent to an AI service.
